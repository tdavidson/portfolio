# Code of conduct

Portfolio is built for and with people who run small funds, and the people who help them. Everyone
taking part here (issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews) is expected to make it a place
where people want to come back.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people here are busy GPs, CFOs and developers doing this on
  the side.
- Be specific and constructive. Critique the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time: search before you post, and say what you tried.
- Protect privacy. Never post real fund, LP, investor or portfolio-company data, or anyone's personal
  details, in an issue, discussion, log or screenshot. Replace them with invented values.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Pitching unrelated products or services, or spam.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove posts, and may block anyone from the project, for behavior
that goes against this code. To report a problem privately, write through
[hemrock.com/contact](https://www.hemrock.com/contact). Reports are kept confidential.
