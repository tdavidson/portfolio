# Fund metrics and capital accounts

## Multiples

With paid-in capital (PIC), cumulative distributions (D) and net asset value (NAV):

| Metric | Formula | Says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DPI | D ÷ PIC | Cash returned per dollar paid in. |
| RVPI | NAV ÷ PIC | Value still held per dollar paid in. |
| TVPI | (D + NAV) ÷ PIC = DPI + RVPI | Total value per dollar paid in. |
| MOIC (gross) | (Realized + unrealized value) ÷ invested cost | The portfolio's multiple, before fees, expenses and carry. |

**Net** multiples use the LPs' paid-in capital and the NAV after accrued carry; **gross** use the
investments' cost and value. State which, every time.

## IRR

IRR is the rate that makes the net present value of dated cash flows zero. For LP net IRR: calls are
negative on their dates, distributions positive on theirs, and the current NAV (after accrued carry)
is a final positive flow on the as-of date. Use XIRR-style calculation with actual dates. Gross IRR
uses the investments' cash flows and current fair values instead.

Early in a fund's life IRR swings widely and is not meaningful on its own; say so when a fund is
under about three years old. A subscription line delays calls and raises IRR; when the fund uses
one, give IRR with and without it if you can.

## Capital account roll-forward

Each partner's account for a period:

| Line | Example LP A |
| --- | --- |
| Beginning balance | 1,000,000 |
| + Contributions | 200,000 |
| − Distributions | (150,000) |
| − Management fees | (12,500) |
| − Fund expenses | (2,500) |
| + Net investment income | 1,000 |
| ± Realized gain or loss | 40,000 |
| ± Change in unrealized gain or loss | 90,000 |
| − Carried interest (accrued or paid) | (26,000) |
| **Ending balance** | **1,140,000** |

Allocate fees by the fee basis, expenses and gains by the LPA's allocation method (usually pro rata
to commitment or capital), and carry by the waterfall. The partners' ending balances should sum to
the fund's NAV; if they don't, find the difference before anything is sent.
