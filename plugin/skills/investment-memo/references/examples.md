# Memo examples (invented)

Each example shows the shape of one memo section. The companies, people and figures are invented; take the shape, never the facts. The bracketed refs stand for the brief's refs, which the saved memo keeps as citations rather than printing.

## Executive summary

Northwind Freight sells routing software to mid-sized shippers and is raising a 6M Series A. The company reports 1.4M in annual recurring revenue across 38 customers, up from 0.6M a year ago [c_12, c_14]; two customer references confirmed the product is in daily use [q_3]. The main questions are whether the sales motion works outside its home region, where it has four customers [c_18], and how much of its growth came from one carrier partnership that ends next year [f_4].

## Traction

Revenue grew from 0.6M to 1.4M ARR over the last twelve months, per the company's monthly figures [c_12]. Net revenue retention was 118%, as reported by management and not yet checked against billing data [c_15]. Gross margin is 71% [c_16]. The company projects 3.5M ARR by the end of next year; that is a projection, built on doubling the sales team [c_21].

## Risks and open questions

- **Customer concentration.** The largest customer is 22% of revenue [c_19]. A second reference call would show how firm that contract is.
- **Partnership expiry.** The carrier partnership behind a third of new customers ends next year [f_4]; management has not said what replaces it.
- **Unverified retention.** Net retention comes only from management's figures [c_15]; a billing export would settle it.
