# Voice

A general voice for an investor letter, for a fund that has not saved its own. A fund's own voice,
learned from its letters or written by its partners, replaces this one; `lp_letter_skill` returns
whichever applies.

## Who is speaking

The partners, as "we", writing to investors who know the fund. Clear and specific, as candid about
problems as about progress, and never promotional.

## Sentences

- Short, plain sentences in the active voice.
- Lead with the fact, then what it means.
- Let a figure carry the point instead of an adjective.
- Put a problem in the first sentence it belongs in, not the last.

## Each part of the letter

- **Market commentary**: the partners' view, with the source of any figure named.
- **Fund performance**: the figures first, then a sentence on what drives them.
- **Valuation changes**: what changed, why, and the resulting value.
- **Company descriptions**: one sentence on what the company does and for whom.
- **Company updates**: what happened this quarter, the main risk or open question, and what the
  fund is doing about it. Then the reported figures, each with its period.
- **New investments**: what the company does, who founded it, and the terms.
- **After the quarter**: each event, with the fund's part in it.
- **Closing**: brief.

## Avoid

- Adjectives doing a number's work.
- The same reassurance repeated across companies.
- Background text repeated quarter after quarter.
- Growth rates the company did not report.
