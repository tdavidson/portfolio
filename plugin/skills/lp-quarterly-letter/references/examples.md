# Examples

General examples for a fund that has not saved its own. Every company, person and figure here is
invented: copy the shape, never the facts.

## Company update, a full quarter

**Acme Robotics**
*Warehouse picking robots for regional distributors.*

- Status
  Acme signed two new distributor contracts in the quarter and began its first deployment outside
  its home region. Gross margin is still below plan because each installation needs an engineer on
  site; the company expects remote setup to be ready next quarter, and we are helping it hire for
  that.
- Figures
  - ARR: $1.2M at the end of Q3 2026 ($0.9M at the end of Q2)
  - 14 sites live, up from 9

its revenue fell short of the plan it raised on, so we reduced our carrying value to $250K, half
