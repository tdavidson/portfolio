## Pinecrest Bio

Pinecrest Bio was written off on May 31, 2026. The company wound down after its lead program failed a safety study. The fund invested $750,000, and the position is now carried at $0.

## Saltmarsh Energy

Saltmarsh had a down round in April: it raised a $6M Series A extension at a lower price per share than our Series A, and we marked the fund's position down from $1,500,000 to $900,000. Two pilot sites are now generating revenue, and the company reported Q2 2026 revenue of $310K. Management estimates the new funding gives it runway into the second half of 2027.

## Lattice Freight

Lattice had its strongest quarter. It added 11 new shipper customers, for 58 in total, and opened its second dispatch hub in the Midwest. The company is starting a search for a VP of Sales in August.
